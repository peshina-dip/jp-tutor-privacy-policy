JP Tutor JP – Privacy Policy

Effective date: November 17, 2026

1. Introduction

JP Tutor JP respects your privacy. This policy explains how information is handled when you use our app.

2. Information We Collect

JP Tutor JP does not require users to create an account. The app does not intentionally collect personal information such as names, email addresses, phone numbers, or location.

3. Camera and Photo Access

The app may request camera or photo gallery access so you can capture or select images containing Japanese text. Images are processed on your device for Japanese text recognition (OCR) and reading conversion. We do not upload your selected images to our own servers.

4. Text Processing and Storage

Recognized Japanese text and readings are processed on your device. Content you choose to save may be stored locally on your device. We do not intentionally transmit this content to our own external servers.

5. Third-Party Services

The app does not currently use advertising services or require third-party accounts. Its OCR and Japanese reading features are designed to work on your device.

6. Data Security

We do not intentionally send your images or recognized text to our servers. You are responsible for protecting files you save or share from your device.

7. Children's Privacy

JP Tutor JP does not knowingly collect personal information from children.

8. Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy when app features or privacy practices change. Updated versions will be published on this page.
